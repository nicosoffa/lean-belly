## COMPLIANCE NOTES FOR ALL VIDEOS

Before uploading to Meta Ads:

| Rule | What to check |
|---|---|
| No before/after side-by-side | Never show two images of same person labeled "before" and "after" |
| No disease claims | Don't say "treats obesity", "cures diabetes", "for diabetics" |
| Attribution required | Any testimonial must have name + location |
| Results disclaimer | Add text: "Results not typical. Individual results may vary." |
| No fake news styling | Don't use news channel logos or breaking news graphics |
| Bridge consistency | Video visual style must match the bridge page at healthreport.today |
| Disclosure | Add "Sponsored" is auto-added by Meta — don't fake organic |
